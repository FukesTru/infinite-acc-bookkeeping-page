What we handle

What does our bookkeeping service include?

Choose ongoing monthly bookkeeping or a focused project, such as a QuickBooks clean-up. Here is what Infinite Accounting Services takes off your desk.

  1. Monthly bookkeeping

    Every transaction recorded and sorted into the right category each month, so your books stay current.

  2. Bank and credit card reconciliation

    Each account matched to its statement every month. Reconciling means checking every entry in your books against the bank's own record.

  3. Financial statements

    Profit and loss statements and balance sheets, ready for you, your lender and tax time. A balance sheet shows what the business owns and owes on a given date.

  4. Cash flow reporting

    A clear view of money coming in and going out, so you can plan for slower months and larger purchases.

  5. Accounts receivable

    Invoices tracked so you know who owes you and when to follow up. Receivables are money customers owe you.

  6. Accounts payable

    Bills organized and scheduled so vendors are paid on time. Payables are money you owe to others.

  7. QuickBooks setup, clean-up and training

    A clean chart of accounts, corrected entries and training for anyone who enters items. QuickBooks is accounting software made by Intuit.

  8. Catch-up bookkeeping

    Months or years of backlogged transactions sorted, reconciled and brought current.

  9. Payroll support

    Payroll records kept organized and reflected correctly in your books [VERIFY].

  10. Books ready for your tax return

    Because we also prepare tax returns, your business and personal returns start from books we have already reconciled.

Questions

What do owners ask before they call us?

Don't see your question? Call or text (708) 232-3003 or read our full FAQ.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping is the day to day recording, sorting and reconciling of your transactions. Accounting builds on those records to produce reports, plan ahead and prepare tax returns. Accurate bookkeeping makes everything that depends on it more reliable.

How often will you update my books?

Most ongoing clients choose monthly bookkeeping. Each month we record your transactions, reconcile your accounts and send reports you can review. Businesses with fewer transactions sometimes choose a quarterly schedule.

How much does bookkeeping cost at Infinite Accounting Services?

Your bookkeeping fee depends on how many accounts and transactions you have. We quote it after a free consultation, before any work begins, and the agreed fee is written in your engagement letter. If your needs change, we talk with you first.

Do you work in QuickBooks, or can I keep my software?

We work in QuickBooks and can set up a new file or clean up an existing one. If you use different software, tell us what you have. We will talk through whether to keep it or move, based on what keeps your records accurate.

Can you catch up several years of bookkeeping?

Yes. We regularly help owners who are months or years behind. We work through statements period by period, reconcile each account and bring your books current. If tax returns are also unfiled, we coordinate both.

Is my financial information kept confidential?

Yes. Your records are used only to provide the services you engage us for, and they stay within our small firm. Please never send Social Security or full account numbers through the website form.

Do you prepare both personal and business tax returns?

Yes. We prepare individual returns and business returns for sole proprietors, LLCs, partnerships and S corporations. Many owners like one firm to handle both because business income often flows onto the personal return. See our tax services.
