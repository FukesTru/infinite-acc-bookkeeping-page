Bookkeeping cleanup

Are your practice's books months or years behind?

It happens to busy practices all the time, and it can be fixed. We bring your books current period by period, so you can see where the practice really stands.

  • We gather your statements and sort every missing transaction, period by period
  • Every bank and credit card account is reconciled and brought up to date
  • Your QuickBooks file is corrected, with a clean chart of accounts (the list of categories your books use)
  • You receive financial statements you can hand to your CPA, partners or lender
  • If tax returns are also behind, we coordinate the tax side

Ongoing accounting

What does ongoing monthly accounting include?

Once your books are clean, we keep them that way. Each month follows the same steady routine, and you see where your practice stands without having to chase anyone for it.

  1. Monthly bookkeeping

    Every transaction recorded and sorted into the right category each month, so your books stay current.

  2. Bank and credit card reconciliation

    Each account matched to its statement every month. Reconciling means checking every entry in your books against the bank's own record.

  3. Monthly financial statements

    A profit and loss statement and a balance sheet, ready for you, your partners, your lender and tax time. A balance sheet shows what the practice owns and owes on a given date.

  4. Cash flow reporting

    A clear view of money coming in and going out, so you can plan for slower months, new hires and larger purchases.

  5. Accounts receivable

    Amounts owed to the practice recorded and tracked in your books, so your reports show what is still outstanding. Receivables are money owed to you.

  6. Accounts payable

    Vendor bills organized and scheduled so suppliers, landlords and contractors are paid on time. Payables are money you owe to others.

  7. Payroll support

    Pay for staff and clinicians recorded correctly in your books, whether they are employees or contractors [VERIFY].

  8. QuickBooks support and training

    Training for anyone on your team who enters items, and answers when something looks wrong. QuickBooks is accounting software made by Intuit.

  9. Books ready for your tax return

    Because we also prepare tax returns, your business and personal returns start from books we have already reconciled.

  10. A person who knows your file

    Questions about a number go to someone who knows your practice, by phone, video call or email.

Questions

What do practice owners ask before they call us?

Don't see your question? Call or text (708) 232-3003 or read our full FAQ.

Do you work with practices outside Illinois?

Yes. Infinite Accounting Services works with healthcare and mental health practices across the United States. Calls are by phone or video, and we work from your statements and accounting software online, so your location does not matter.

What is a bookkeeping cleanup?

A bookkeeping cleanup brings books that are behind or full of errors up to date. We sort and categorize the missing transactions, reconcile every account to its statements and correct your QuickBooks file, so your financial statements are accurate again.

How long does a bookkeeping cleanup take?

It depends on how many months are behind and how many accounts your practice has. After we review your books, we tell you what the work involves before anything begins.

How much do cleanup and monthly accounting cost?

Your fee depends on the size of the cleanup and on how many accounts and transactions your practice has each month. We quote it after your first call, before any work begins, and the agreed fee is written in your engagement letter.

Will you need my patients' health information?

Bookkeeping works from your bank and card statements, payroll records and accounting software. Please never send patient names, health information or Social Security numbers through the website form or by email.

Do you work in QuickBooks, or can I keep my software?

We work in QuickBooks and can set up a new file or clean up an existing one. If you use different software, tell us what you have. We will talk through whether to keep it or move, based on what keeps your records accurate.

Is my practice's financial information kept confidential?

Yes. Your records are used only to provide the services you engage us for, and they stay within our small firm. Please never send Social Security or full account numbers through the website form.

Do you prepare tax returns for practice owners?

Yes. We prepare business returns for sole proprietors, LLCs, partnerships and S corporations, and individual returns for owners. Many owners like one firm to handle both, because practice income often flows onto the personal return. See our tax services.
